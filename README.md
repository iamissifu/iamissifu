## About

I work on the security of intelligent systems: how models, the platforms they run on, and the physical processes they sense can fail, and how to keep that failure observable and contained.

My background sits across software engineering, cybersecurity, IoT, and machine learning. Day to day that looks like building and assessing AI agents and RAG services, hardening cloud and Kubernetes environments, and applying sensing and analytics in agricultural and mining settings. I also teach, review technical work, and run a beginner CTF platform.

---

## Focus

**AI systems.** Retrieval, tool-using agents, evaluation, tracing, and application-layer controls (input, tool, and output policy). Threat modeling against OWASP LLM Top 10 and MITRE ATLAS. Governance mapping to NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act, and ISO/IEC 42001.

**Cloud and infrastructure.** IAM, isolation, sandbox labs, Terraform on AWS, Kubernetes baselines, logging, and runtime integrity.

**IoT and industrial environments.** Sensing, telemetry, and applied ML for smart agriculture and mining, including production forecasting and environmental monitoring.

**Investigation and assurance.** Digital forensics, OSINT, application security, and information-security audit (ISO/IEC 27001).
